# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Problem Statement
Thousands of students graduate from colleges every year, but many lack awareness of industry expectations, career opportunities, internships, certifications, and suitable employment pathways. Educational institutions struggle to provide personalized career guidance at the necessary scale. There is an urgent need to bridge the gap between campus learning and industry readiness.

## 2. Target Audience
- **Students / Graduates:** Seeking clarity on career paths, skills, and employment opportunities.
- **Educational Institutions:** Looking for scalable ways to offer personalized career guidance.
- **Employers (Private & Government):** Seeking job-ready candidates.

## 3. Core Features (AI-Enabled)

The platform must provide an AI-driven, personalized experience for every student. Core capabilities include:

1. **Profile Analysis:** 
   - Analyze student profiles and academic records.
   - Extract key strengths, weaknesses, and academic inclinations.
2. **Career & Skill Guidance:**
   - **Recommend:** Suitable career paths based on the student's profile.
   - **Suggest:** Specific skill gaps that need to be addressed for their chosen career path.
3. **Learning & Certifications:**
   - **Recommend:** Online courses and certifications to bridge the identified skill gaps.
4. **Opportunity Matching:**
   - **Match:** Internships and private job opportunities tailored to the user's profile and skills.
   - **Recommend:** Government employment opportunities.
5. **Career Tools:**
   - **Generate:** AI-based resume improvements (e.g., parsing existing resumes and suggesting better phrasing, formatting, or keyword optimization).
   - **Conduct:** AI-driven mock interviews (simulating HR or technical rounds and providing instant feedback).

## 4. Expected Outcome
The successful implementation of this platform will lead to improved employability among graduates and successfully realize the vision of **Campus to Corporate**.
